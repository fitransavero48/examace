"Evaluate whether raising the National Minimum Wage is the best way to reduce poverty in the UK."

STUDENT ANSWER A higher minimum wage raises the income of low-paid workers, which can lift households above the relative poverty line of 60% of median income.Knowledge ✓ In sectors like hospitality, where many workers are paid at the minimum, the effect on take-home pay could be large.Application ✓ However, firms may respond by cutting hours or jobs. So the minimum wage is not the best policy.Judgement not supported
